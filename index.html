About

More than a comedian.
A movement.

From the streets of Port Harcourt to stages across the continent, KOBABA Jornsen has built a reputation as one of Nigeria's most magnetic entertainers and cultural convenors. As the CEO of Talkaholic Unlimited, he blends comedy, enterprise and faith into a single mission: raising people, brands and communities.
